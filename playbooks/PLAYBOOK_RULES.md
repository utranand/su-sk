## 1. Business Rules Overview (`playbook-business-rules.md`)

Contains **only business logic checking** for the Smart Utilities domain, consolidated into 9 unified rule definitions:

- **`PBR-01`: Invoice Lifecycle & Mutation Scope (Unpaid, Unprinted, Status Flow & Demographic Mutability):** Rate adjustments apply **ONLY to unprinted, unpaid bills** (`status = 'invoice_status_1'`, `receipt_id IS NULL`, `printed_date IS NULL`). Status lifecycle flows from newly created (`invoice_status_1` รอพิมพ์ใบแจ้งหนี้) to printed (`invoice_status_2` รอชำระ), paid (`invoice_status_3` ชำระแล้ว), or overdue (`invoice_status_4` ค้างชำระ via automated cron). If any candidate bill was paid or printed, financial amounts are strictly immutable and **execution must HALT immediately**. Historical paid periods with receipts must remain completely untouched. However, non-financial demographic metadata (name, address, tax ID, deed no) **CAN be updated** on printed/paid records without reconciliation impact. *(Consolidates former PBR-01, PBR-05, PBR-07, PBR-09)*
- **`PBR-02`: Billing Model Invariants: Accumulated vs Non-Accumulated (Period Editability, Due Date Extension & Two-Tier Architecture):** For accumulated billing (water), arrears roll over; updates are restricted to the **current active month only**, and due dates (`payment_due_date`) must **NEVER be extended** because QR codes are stateless and query the bank directly (checking only expiry + paid status), which would allow old and new bills to be paid simultaneously leading to double payments (ชำระซ้ำซ้อน) or overlapped reconciliation failure. **Two-Tier Architecture:** Every unpaid historical month MUST maintain an immutable **Monthly Assessment Statement (ทะเบียนคุมหนี้/หนังสือแจ้งการประเมิน)** invoice for municipal audit and statutory itemized breakdown, while the latest bill serves as the **Current Active Payable Statement (ใบแจ้งหนี้สำหรับชำระเงินที่ทบยอด)**. Anti-double payment safety is guaranteed because past bills carry expired due dates (`invoice_status_4`) which bank gateways reject at scan time. For non-accumulated billing (garbage), rounds are discrete month-by-month; any unpaid unprinted month can be updated independently and due date extension is permitted. *(Consolidates former PBR-02, PBR-10, PBR-12)*
- **`PBR-03`: Fiscal Year Cycle & Cross-Year Boundary Lock:** Fiscal year ends September 30. Rounds in Oct–Dec have `tax_year = round_year + 1`; rounds in Jan–Sep have `tax_year = round_year`. Public revenues and budgets cannot cross fiscal years. While amounts within a fiscal year (e.g. September) can be modified, they **CANNOT be modified, canceled, or shifted to be paid across into October (the next fiscal year)**. In multi-round adjustments, each billing round must be pinned strictly to its own respective accounting `tax_year`. *(Consolidates former PBR-06, PBR-11)*
- **`PBR-04`: Multi-Tenant Municipality Payer Disambiguation & Strict Scope Confinement:** Customer numbers (`68000977`) are shared across municipalities. Queries and updates must resolve and pin the unique entity UUID (`garbage_id` / `water_id`) and row primary keys. **Municipality Scope Confinement:** When the Jira card assigns a municipality (e.g. `[เกาะสมุย]`), the entire scope of work, investigation, verification (PBR-01..PBR-06), and correction is strictly confined within that municipality. Never evaluate, inspect, or mutate data across other municipalities. *(Former PBR-03)*
- **`PBR-05`: Multi-Channel Satang Synchronization:** Updating fees requires atomic synchronization of complete final PromptPay QR and barcode amount tokens in satang (150baht=15000satang; never last-four-character truncation) (`garbage_fee_qrcode.qr_code_text`, `barcode_text`) in the same transaction so payment gateways charge the exact business rate. *(Former PBR-04)*
- **`PBR-06`: Unpaid Invoice Cancellation by Guarded Deletion:** Cancellation means deleting the exact verified unpaid invoice rows, corresponding exclusively linked unpaid charge transactions, exclusive QR/barcode rows and mapping links in one guarded transaction, rather than assigning a guessed cancellation status. A future due date alone is not a blocker; explicitly verify removal instead of assuming a cascade. Preserve paid/shared/unrelated transactions, receipts and bank evidence; halt on payment, printing or non-target dependency conflicts. Verify canceled charge IDs are absent from owner collection and the remaining balance matches the approved projection. Include full transaction backup/restore and re-ground any transaction-only follow-up from immutable original invoice/reference evidence. Overdue does not imply paid; verify current payment/printing state, actual relationships, exact entity/primary keys and expected counts. Never delete paid invoices or receipt/bank accounting records. Require immutable offline backup, guarded atomic transaction, recovery and explicit per-action approval. Do not use `invoice_status_2` as canceled in the garbage workflow.
- **`PBR-07`: Demographic Data Integrity: Prohibition of Synthetic Citizen Records (Import-Only Invariant):** Operational playbooks, incident handlers, and ad-hoc database corrections are **strictly prohibited from adding new demographic records (`public.demographic`) via SQL `INSERT`**. Demographic entities represent verified physical/legal persons in civil registration and **must enter the system via the official civil/demographic import pipeline only**. Never insert placeholder, synthetic, or dummy citizen entities (e.g. placeholder citizen names or numbers) into production databases to bypass missing-relation crashes or work around frontend/backend null dereference errors. When an entity references a missing or orphaned `demo_id`, the issue must be resolved by: (1) importing the verified official citizen record via the official municipality demographic import process, (2) re-linking to an existing verified citizen record if proven, or (3) hardening application code (frontend optional chaining, backend null handling) to handle missing demographic relations safely.
- **`PBR-08`: Total Water Amount & Tariff Calculation Invariant (Usage Fee, Meter Maintenance, VAT & Rolling Arrears):** Water tariff calculations must strictly follow empirical master data (`master_water_usage_fee_rate`, `master_water_meter`, `master_water_type`). Volume usage is strictly $\text{current} - \text{previous}$. Water usage fee is determined by tier boundaries (flat/step rate per municipality tariff); meter maintenance fee is fixed by meter size; VAT is strictly calculated as $\text{ROUND}((\text{water\_fee} + \text{maint\_fee}) \times 7\%, 2)$; total current water fee is $\text{before\_vat} + \text{vat}$; invoice billing integrates any linked garbage fee and rolls forward unpaid previous month arrears per PBR-02. QR/barcode satang must synchronize atomically per PBR-05.
- **`PBR-09`: Selective Historical Invoice Correction & Incident-Driven Recreation Invariant:** Historical selective-month invoices cannot be cancelled, deleted, or recreated through the standard application UI/API because `getIsWaterInvoiceCancelable` strictly blocks cancellation of non-latest rounds, and API generation auto-increments invoice numbers (`invoice_no` drift) and calculates future due dates (`paymentDueDate = NOW() + 15 days`, violating PBR-02). Therefore, selective historical invoice deletion and recreation must be executed as a guarded database incident using TypeORM-aligned SQL in an atomic transaction that preserves original invoice numbers, maintains original due dates, initializes newly recreated replacement invoices with `status = 'invoice_status_1'` (รอพิมพ์ใบแจ้งหนี้) and `printed_date = NULL` for re-printing (or explicitly sets `invoice_status_4` when immediate overdue presentation is required), strictly populates valid `template_id` and authentic municipal officer `created_by` (never card keys/placeholders) to prevent NestJS API 500 crashes during template rendering, synchronizes QR/barcode satang (PBR-05), and preserves integrated billing (PBR-06).

## 2. Architect Rules Overview (`playbook-architect-rules.md`)

Contains **architecture, development context, code security, and execution safety** that do not belong to business logic. Every playbook referencing `playbook-architect-rules.md` must adhere to all rules below:

- **`PAR-01`: Zero DDL Floor (Application-User Least Privilege):** The prod app DB user has no `CREATE TABLE` / `ALTER TABLE` privileges. Pure DML only. Pre-fix snapshots are exported to offline JSON (`backup_data_<date>.json`); rollback is scripted as pure DML (`05_rollback.sql`).
- **`PAR-02`: Guarded Atomic Transaction:** All mutations run inside a single transaction (`BEGIN ... ROLLBACK/COMMIT`) with an upfront PL/pgSQL assertion block (`DO $$ ... $$;`) asserting expected row counts before modifying data. Aborts with `RAISE EXCEPTION` on mismatch.
- **`PAR-03`: Mandatory Dry-Run (Rollback Default):** Correction scripts must default to ending in `ROLLBACK;`. Verify in-transaction readback preview during dry-run before changing to `COMMIT;`.
- **`PAR-04`: Human Sign-Off Gate (Production Write Safety):** Governed by framework Rule 4. A production database mutation is **NEVER autonomous**. Live commit requires explicit human approval.
- **`PAR-05`: Primary Key Specificity & Blast-Radius Audit:** Every update must pin explicit row primary key UUIDs and include Jira ticket audit remarks (`remarks = '<CARD_KEY> ...'`).
- **`PAR-06`: Zero-SQL Executive Reporting (Thai):** Produce a non-technical executive incident report (`INCIDENT_REPORT_TH.md`) with prominent target entity UUID and zero raw SQL code.
- **`PAR-07`: Complete Workspace & Jira Audit Trail:** Maintain isolated feature workspace, standard 5-script suite, offline JSON backup, syntax-highlighted footprints on Jira, and synchronize the Business Rules Decision Gate directly into the task description's Definition of Ready (DoR) block.
